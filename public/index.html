⚡ Bounded Queue Backpressure

Explicit drop_newest (429 Too Many Requests) and drop_oldest policies prevent out-of-memory crashes on resource-constrained devices.

🎯 Pluggable Inference Adapters

Decoupled capture and inference abstractions allow hot-swapping between YOLO v8 ONNX models and deterministic fixture adapters.

📶 Resilient Offline Forwarding

Buffers detection envelopes during connectivity outages and drains to cloud ingestion APIs automatically upon network restoration.
